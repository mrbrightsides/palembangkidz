
# Visual Direction: Whimsical 3D Claymation Guide

To maintain a consistent, high-quality "PalembangKidz" aesthetic across your videos and images, use the following prompt frameworks:

## Image Generation (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E 3)
**Prompt Structure:**
> `A [SUBJECT] in Palembang, Indonesia, designed in a high-detail 3D claymation style, whimsical and friendly, soft studio lighting, vibrant colors (sky blue, golden yellow, crimson), clay textures with slight fingerprints, rounded corners, isometric view, 4k, rendered in Octane.`

**Examples:**
- *Pempek:* `A plate of Pempek fish cakes and a small bowl of dark chocolate-brown vinegar sauce (cuko), 3D claymation style, vibrant, cute, food photography style.`
- *Ampera Bridge:* `Ampera Bridge Palembang at sunset, simplified 3D clay architecture, soft glowing lights on the bridge, stylized Musi River below, whimsical clouds, 3D claymation style.`

## Video Generation (e.g., Luma, Runway Gen-2)
**Prompt Structure:**
> `3D claymation stop-motion animation of [ACTION/SUBJECT]. The clay moves with a charming, slightly jerky stop-motion feel. Soft clay textures, bright and cheerful environment, 1080p, high frame rate but stylized movement.`

**Key Visual Keywords:**
- *Textures:* Soft clay, matte plastic, doughy, organic.
- *Lighting:* Diffuse, warm, "sunny morning" vibe.
- *Physics:* Squash and stretch, bouncy, playful.
- *Colors:* Sky Blue (#e0f2fe), Palembang Gold (#facc15), Heritage Red (#ef4444).
